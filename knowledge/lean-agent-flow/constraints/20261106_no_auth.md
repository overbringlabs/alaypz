# No authentication

Given by the operator 2026-11-06 ("you do not need mix phx.gen.auth").

- Player identity = the name typed once per browser, persisted in
  `localStorage`.
- The server-side play session is tied to the LiveView connection (anonymous
  client); when the connection dies the session is abandoned after a grace
  period (S6).
- Consequence: identity does not follow a player across devices or browsers
  unless they carry their access string.
- Names are globally unique; a one-time 20-char access string (persisted as a
  salted hash) lets the original owner reclaim their name — still no accounts
  (D-20261106-02).
